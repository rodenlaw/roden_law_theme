## What signing means

Each row is one statement of Georgia law that Roden pages may make, with the authority it rests on.
When you sign a row, two things follow:

- **New Roden content may make the claim**, citing that authority. Until a row is signed, no lane
  (blog pipeline, refreshes, FAQs, practice pages) may add a new Georgia claim resting on it.
- **The site sweep checks every page against the row.** A page that says something different,
  or uses a figure not in the "figures" column, is flagged as an error.

Please answer each row **approve** or **correct**. For a correction, give the wording you want;
it comes back to you once more before it counts. Replying "approve all" to a section is fine. If
any row is outside your practice, say so and we will route it elsewhere.

The primary-text sources each statement was checked against are on file and can be sent with any
row.
