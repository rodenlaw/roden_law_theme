## Why now

The troubled-teen abuse page (`/class-action-lawyers/troubled-teen-industry-abuse/`) tells readers
that abuse-survivor rules "often extend the window" to file. As we read § 15-3-555, the extended
period covers **sexual abuse or incest**. Physical abuse and neglect, which the page also lists, run
on the ordinary deadline, tolled for minority under § 15-3-40. The corrected FAQ waits on these two
rows. The site sweep already flags the overbroad wording as an error.

Code § 15-3-555 | sexual abuse and incest limitation period | An action to recover damages for injury arising out of an act of sexual abuse or incest must be commenced within six years after the person turns 21, or within three years after the person discovers the injury and its causal relationship to the sexual abuse or incest, whichever occurs later ((A)).
