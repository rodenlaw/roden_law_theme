## South Carolina: Graeham C. Gillin

- **1. UM/UIM stacking (NC-G3 resolved).** Certain insureds may stack, subject to § 38-77-160.
  - **Approved page wording:** "South Carolina permits certain insureds to stack UM/UIM coverage.
    Generally, a Class I insured (such as the named insured, spouse, or resident relative) may stack
    available coverage when an insured vehicle is involved, subject to S.C. Code § 38-77-160,
    including the limit based on the coverage carried on the vehicle involved in the accident. If none
    of the insured's vehicles is involved, coverage is generally limited to the coverage available on
    one vehicle and stacking is not permitted."
- **2(a). Ordinary wrongful death.** Confirmed: § 15-3-530(6) gives three years beginning at the
  death. "3 years from the date of death" stands, and T-WD1 needs no change.
- **2(b). Malpractice death (WD-G1 / B3-G3 resolved).**
  - The medical-malpractice limitations provisions govern, including § 15-3-545. The deadline depends
    on when the treatment occurred and when the claim was or reasonably should have been
    discovered, with a six-year repose.
  - The wrongful death period does not revive a malpractice claim that was already barred before
    the death. "From the date of death" is wrong for malpractice deaths (1646, 4350, 4101).
- **2(c). Government claims.** § 15-78-110 generally gives two years after the loss was or should
  have been discovered, or potentially three years if the statutory claim procedure is used first.
- **3. Funeral costs and creditors (WD-G3 resolved).** Both statements are correct, with refined
  wording.
  - **Funeral:** "reasonable funeral expenses may be recovered as part of the wrongful death claim,
    provided they are not also recovered in the survival action" (§ 15-5-100).
  - **Creditors:** "wrongful death proceeds generally are not assets of the decedent's estate
    available to satisfy the decedent's general creditors" (§§ 15-51-20, 15-51-40). Avoid an absolute
    "creditors cannot reach them".
- **4. Nursing-home caps (WD-G4 resolved).** Approved page wording: "Damage limitations may apply in
  some nursing home cases. South Carolina's medical malpractice noneconomic damages limits can apply
  to qualifying malpractice claims against nursing homes and health care providers, and separate
  statutory limits may apply to entities that qualify as charitable organizations. Whether a cap
  applies depends on the defendant, the nature of the claim, and any statutory exception."
- **5. Helmets (MC-G1 / B3-G4 resolved).** Cut the comparative-fault helmet lines back to the statute.
  Approved page wording: "South Carolina law requires motorcycle operators and passengers under age
  21 to wear an approved helmet. S.C. Code § 56-5-3660. State law does not impose that helmet
  requirement on riders age 21 or older."
