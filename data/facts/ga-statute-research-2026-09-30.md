## Confirmed from statute text

| Section | What the text says | Source | Fixes / unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **§ 40-8-76** (child restraints) | A child **under eight** must be in an appropriate federally approved child passenger restraining system **in a rear seat**. Front seat only if there is no appropriate rear seat or all are occupied by other children. **Over 4'9"**: a safety belt instead (§ 40-8-76.1). A child **≥40 lb** may use a lap belt only if lap-and-shoulder belts are absent or all (except the driver's) are used by other children. Fines **up to $50** first, **up to $100** after; no added fees. **(c)** Not negligence per se; a (b) violation cannot be used to cancel coverage or raise rates. Excludes taxis and public transit. No points stated. | FindLaw (2024-03-28) | The Georgia car-seat guide's core content (ages, rear seat, height, penalty) |
| **§ 40-6-315** (helmets) | "No person shall operate or ride upon a motorcycle unless he or she is wearing protective headgear which complies with standards established by the commissioner of public safety." **Universal, no age exception.** Exempt: enclosed cab or motorized cart, and three-wheeled agricultural-use motorcycles. | Justia (2024) | Confirms the live motorcycle pillar text; the GA statewide motorcycle page can state it |
| **§ 51-3-1** (premises) | An owner or occupier who invites others onto the premises "is liable in damages to such persons for injuries caused by his failure to exercise ordinary care in keeping the premises and approaches safe." | Justia (2024) | Guide 4 (the duty only) |
| **§ 51-4-2** (wrongful death, spouse or parent) | "The surviving spouse or, if there is no surviving spouse, a child or children, either minor or sui juris, may recover … the full value of the life of the decedent." Spouse and children share per capita, but "the surviving spouse shall receive **no less than one-third** of such recovery." | FindLaw (2024-03-28) | **PB-WD3:** the live FAQ inverts the one-third rule. **T-WD2 / M-WD1:** cite and priority. |
| **§ 51-4-5** (estate) | If no one may sue under §§ 51-4-2 or 51-4-4, the administrator or executor sues for the next of kin. Separately: "the personal representative … shall be entitled to recover for the **funeral, medical, and other necessary expenses** resulting from the injury and death." | Justia (2024) | The estate claim; WD pages and guides |
| **§ 9-3-71 / 72 / 73** (med mal) | Two years from the date the injury or death occurred, five-year repose, no general discovery rule. Foreign object: one year after discovery. Minors under five: until two years after the fifth birthday, repose at age ten. | Justia (2020) | Done 2026-09-29 (GA pack #73); **GA-B3-2** table cells |
| **§ 9-11-9.1** (expert affidavit) | (a) An expert affidavit is filed with the complaint. (b) If the limitation period expires within 10 days: attorney affidavit, then the expert affidavit within **45 days** (not extendable without consent). (e) An allegedly **defective** affidavit may be **cured by amendment within 30 days** of service of the motion to dismiss. | Justia (2024), FindLaw | **M-MM2:** "results in immediate dismissal" / "will dismiss" overstates this. Proposed: "can be dismissed". |
| **§ 51-1-29.5** (emergency care) | For claims from emergency medical care in a hospital emergency department or obstetrical unit (or a surgical suite right after ED evaluation), "no physician or health care provider shall be held liable unless it is proven by **clear and convincing evidence** that … [the] actions showed **gross negligence**." "Emergency medical care" is bona fide emergency services after an acute condition, **excluding** care after stabilization and care unrelated to the original emergency. | Justia (2024) | **GA-4:** the ER page's "does not lower the fundamental duty" is wrong for GA |
| **§ 40-6-91** (crosswalk) | "The driver of a vehicle shall stop and remain stopped to allow a pedestrian to cross the roadway within a crosswalk when the pedestrian is upon the half of the roadway upon which the vehicle is traveling, or … within one lane of [that] half …." | Justia (2024) | **M-PED1:** the crosswalk duty is § 40-6-91. The "due care … any roadway" duty is § 40-6-93 (text not yet retrieved). |
| **§ 34-9-11(a)** (exclusive remedy) | Comp remedies "exclude and be in place of all other rights and remedies …". An employee may sue a third-party tort-feasor "**other than an employee of the same employer** or any person who … provides workers' compensation benefits". | FindLaw (2024-03-28) | **GA-2:** co-worker immunity |
| **§ 34-9-263** (PPD schedule) | Rating × weeks × TTD rate. Arm **225**, leg **225**, hand **160**, foot **135**, one eye **150**, body as a whole **300** (statute). Handbook also lists thumb 60, fingers 40/35/25/15, great toe 30, other toe 10, hearing 75 (one ear) / 150 (both). | Justia (2024); SBWC employee handbook | Guide 5. Confirm the handbook figures against the statute. |
| **§§ 34-9-261 / -262** (weekly caps) | TTD **$800 max / $50 min** for injuries on or after **2023-07-01**; TPD **$533** for up to **350 weeks**. | SBWC employee handbook; Justia § 34-9-261/-262 | Guide 5. Confirm no later change. |
| **SBWC Rule 61** (WC-1) | The employer completes WC-1 Section A on learning of the injury. The insurer files with the Board **within 21 days** of the employer's knowledge of disability, for cases with **more than 7 days** of lost time (≤7 days: WC-26). | SBWC rules and procedure manual | **GA-1:** the live "must be filed within 10 days" looks wrong |
