## Situation

The local-SEO autorun has been paused since **2026-08-28** for the legal-accuracy remediation,
and the pause note reads "content generation resumes on request" — a soft hold, not a gate
(`data/local-seo/autorun.state.json`). This client has **no `recovery.paused` switch at all**:
`config.overlay.json` carries no `localSeo.recovery` block and `autorun-ctl.mjs status` reports
the recovery guard clear, so nothing structural stands behind the pause. The site is
nonetheless inside a declared cleanup whose success metric is **positions 1–3 holding or
climbing through the next core update, baseline 68** (Semrush US database, Jul 2026), and whose
standing rule is to judge nothing between update boundaries — the Aug 2026 spam update began
8/18 and was still rolling at audit time, and the next core update has not landed. GSC confirms
the trajectory rather than contradicting it: **1,191 clicks in Jul 2026 and 825 through 8/22**,
tracking to roughly 1,150 and the lowest full month in the 13-month window (snapshot
2026-08-24). Both law packs are **UNSIGNED** — GA with 22 authorities and 22 unverified, SC with
25 and 25 — so no lane may produce a new legal claim citing their authorities, and the statute
tracker still shows **201 statutes and 410 page-citations unverified** against primary text
(2026-09-03), against a 2026-08-26 audit that found **32 pages carrying a false statement of
law**. The decisive fact for the automated lane is narrower and worse: on **2026-09-08** a
recheck found **31 instances across 23 pages** still citing S.C. Code § 15-38-15 for the
plaintiff's recovery bar — a class already corrected in #97 — *including a dozen local-SEO blog
posts that postdate the fix*, which is direct evidence that the pipeline's templates carry
corrected errors forward. Site health is overall **FAIL** as of 2026-09-10, with the doorway
ratio at **40.9% of 1,230 indexable URLs against a 25% ceiling** and the granularity floor
failing on **162 errors**, while the cull has taken location + practice-area URLs from 668 to
538 against a 418–535 target band. The autorun's own last output on 2026-08-24 was
`wando-gardens-faber-place-drive-best-car-accident-lawyer-north-charleston` — a subdivision and
a street below the incorporated-municipality floor the site-health check is failing on.

## What would change this

**hyperlocal** — resumes when all four hold: (1) the 32 false-law pages are remediated and the
count is recorded; (2) both law packs are signed, or the lane is re-scoped to produce no new
statutory claims at all; (3) the § 15-38-15 class re-check comes back clean against posts
published after the fix, proving the templates no longer carry it forward; and (4) the lane's
targeting is raised to the incorporated-municipality floor, so it stops emitting subdivision
and street slugs while the doorway ratio is above 25%. A fall in Savannah's green-pin share
below 38%, or positions 1–3 dropping below 68 after a completed core update, would raise the
cost of the pause and argue for re-scoping the lane sooner — but not for resuming it unchanged.

**referenceLayer** — goes off if the attorney review does not land, or if review shows the
drafted guides depend on unverified pack authorities after all. Expands if the packs are signed.

**researchAssets** — goes off if no dataset survives the Study #1 verification standard (every
prose figure checked programmatically against the shipped data). A second study that cannot be
verified should not ship at all rather than ship softened.

**spanish** — turns on the moment an English reference or research page is attorney-approved
and published, and not before: the twin must never lead the English original.

**gbp** — turns on with hyperlocal, or sooner if the reference layer or a research asset
publishes something worth a hand-scheduled post. A Myrtle Beach address decision would also
reopen it for that market specifically.

**freshness** — turns on when the statistics registry has a verified, dated source for every
figure it would refresh, and when a dry run proves the tool can see all four surfaces
(`post_content`, `_roden_faqs`, `_roden_key_takeaways`, `post_excerpt`). The January CPI
adjustment to S.C. Code § 15-32-220 is the first real job waiting for it.
