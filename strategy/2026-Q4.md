## What would change this

**hyperlocal** turns back on only when all of these hold:
1. Both packs are signed. GA must be signed by Love *for Roden*.
2. The statute item is closed or no longer blocking.
3. A claims sweep PASSes on the day of resume.
4. The lane's targeting is shown to stop at the incorporated-municipality floor.

Then the owner clears `recovery.paused`, and the spend cap returns to 12 in the same plays file.
Positions 1–3 falling below 68 after a completed core update, or Savannah falling below 38% on
Local Dominator, would raise the cost of the pause. That argues for re-scoping the lane sooner,
not for resuming it unchanged.

**referenceLayer** goes off for any guide whose claims turn out to rest on unsigned GA
authorities. The GA workers' comp guide publishes the day Love signs the authorities it cites.

**researchAssets:** the GA report of Study #2 publishes on Love's read. The lane goes off if no
further dataset meets the Study #1 standard (every prose figure checked programmatically).

**spanish** widens only when another English reference or research page is attorney-approved
and published. A twin never leads its original.

**gbp** turns on with hyperlocal, or once the profile links are clean and a published research
or reference page is worth a hand-scheduled post. Columbia stays out until its profile is
verified.

**freshness** turns on when the statistics registry has a verified, dated source for every
figure it would refresh, a dry run proves it can see all four surfaces, and the live sweep
parses cleanly. The January CPI adjustment to S.C. Code § 15-32-220 is the first job waiting
for it.
