## 3. The Local-Pack artefact, and the GBP link change

The homepage shows 118,544 non-brand impressions for "personal injury lawyer", "car accident
lawyer", "medical malpractice lawyer" at **position 1.3–1.5 with 0 clicks**. That is not a
page-one organic ranking. It is the Local Pack: Google reports the GBP website link's URL at the
pack's position, and pack clicks go to the profile, not the site.

On 2026-08-21 (#54) the GBP links were retargeted from the homepage to the office pages with a
`?ref=gmb_*` tag. GSC shows the handover exactly: homepage impressions 9,236 → 511/wk and the
`?ref=` URLs 5,686 → 13,070/wk in the same week. The Charleston `?ref=gmb_chs` URL now carries
41,279 impressions at position 2.0, the Savannah one 11,467 at 6.1.

Verified live 2026-09-23: `?ref=gmb_chs` and `?ref=gmb_sav` **301 to the clean office URL**;
the old `?utm_campaign=` form returns 200 with a canonical to the clean URL. Nothing to fix.
GSC will keep reporting the pack under the tagged URL because that is what the profile links to.

**Consequence for every number in this document:** subtract the home + `?ref=` + locations
group to see organic. Organic-only: **2,753 clicks on 1.28M impressions now, 4,404 on 2.20M
before.** Same −37%.

# Addendum, 2026-09-25 — the Aug 21 AI-impression collapse (item 8)

**Finding: it is the August 2026 spam update, not a deploy.** Google's spam update ran
**Aug 18 → Aug 21** (Search Engine Roundtable, September webmaster report). The AI series breaks
on exactly those days: Aug 18 1,091 · 19 792 · 20 575 · 21 410 · 22 182. The Aug 12–13 AI spike
(2,011/day) sits on the Aug 12–13 ranking volatility the same report records. This is the
**second spam update in a row that has hit this site**: the August 2025 one took out the
pain-and-suffering post (§B).

## What was ruled out

| Hypothesis | Test | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `2787ffa` heading/schema change (08-19) | Read the diff | On blog posts it demoted sidebar and footer `h2` → `h3` only. Schema change was `aggregateRating` on home/office pages. Nothing in the article body or Article schema. |
| The "hundreds of reviews" DB sweep (`9a9ea8c`, 123 bodies, 08-19/20) | Revision dates on prod for the 20 most-cited posts | **No body edit Aug 18–22 on 19 of 20.** Only myrtle-beach (08-19 23:02). |
| Snippet or robots directives | Git history `-S nosnippet/max-snippet`; live headers and meta | None, ever. robots.txt allows everything. |
| Site opted out of AI features | Search Console → Settings → Search generative AI | **Include.** |
| Manual action | Search Console → Manual actions | **No issues detected.** |
| Reporting artefact | Per-page web data (`ai-cited-pages-daily-web.csv`) | The cited posts lost their position-2–4 web impressions **on Aug 20**, the same day. `continuing-post-accident-medical-treatment`: 117 impr @ 3 on Aug 19 → 16 @ 42 on Aug 20. The AI Overview citation is also counted as a web impression at the Overview's position, so this is the same event seen twice. |

## What to do

1. **Nothing to revert.** No deploy or data change is implicated. Don't undo the heading
   demotion or the reviews cleanup.
2. **Keep the scaled-content cull moving. It is the only lever that fits this cause.** Spam
   classifiers re-evaluate over months, usually at the next spam or core update. The Oct 20 /
   Nov 17 checkpoints (§10.1) should now also read the Generative AI report: success is AI
   impressions for blog pages coming back toward 500+/day.
3. **Keep up the freshness loop on the cited explainers.** Those 20 posts are the queue. The

4. **Export the Generative AI report weekly.** It's UI-only, and the 16-month window will

5. **Track 10 fixed queries by hand** (the top cited posts' head questions), recording whether
