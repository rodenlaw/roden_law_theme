## 1. What wins

- **PI head terms go to the homepage of a firm based in that city.** Multi-office firms place only with a strong city hub (Farah and HLM `/savannah/`, Joye `/myrtle-beach/`, Sink `/columbia/`).
- **Car, truck and motorcycle terms go to a dedicated P×C page** in Savannah, Columbia and Myrtle Beach. In Charleston, local-HQ firms win with practice pages that don't name the city. A two-state, city-less pillar wins none of these terms.
- **Workers' comp goes to specialist firms' homepages plus directories.**
- **SuperLawyers or Justia take one or two slots in almost every SERP.** The same directories dominate the AI answers in Radar.
- **Breadth doesn't pay.**
  - Steinberg's ~100 programmatic town pages: best one earns 0.45% of its traffic.
  - Joye and Sink practice×city pages: 0.4–1.6% each.
  - Trey and Maguire: ~80% of traffic lands on Google Business Profile-tagged URLs (`?utm=gmb`), so it comes from Maps-pack visibility.

  What wins is **depth on a few pages**: the homepage, the city hub the Business Profile links to, and a handful of P×C pages.
