Blunt Force Trauma – What Do These Injuries Look Like?

Blunt force trauma injuries are those caused by a sudden impact or collision with an object that does not penetrate the skin. This type of injury is common in collisions that cause the body to absorb the impact of immense force from various directions. Victims may suffer from visible bruising, swelling and abrasions. The more severe damage, however, often lies beneath the surface. Blunt force trauma can damage your bones, muscles, organs and internal systems.

How Do Blunt Force Trauma Injuries Occur?

Blunt force trauma injuries typically occur during car crashes when a vehicle stops abruptly or is struck by another vehicle. The sudden deceleration forces the body to collide with hard surfaces like the dashboard, steering wheel or something else.  Even the deployment of airbags, while lifesaving, could cause blunt force injuries. This potential result is due to the speed and force at which they inflate. Cyclists or pedestrians struck by vehicles may also suffer blunt force trauma from the impact.

What Are the Most Common Blunt Force Trauma Injuries?

The most common blunt force trauma injuries in car accidents include:
  • Contusions (Bruises): Blood vessels break under the skin, causing discoloration and pain.
  • Fractures: Bones can break or crack under the force of impact, leading to significant pain and potential complications.
  • Internal Bleeding: Damage to internal organs and blood vessels can lead to dangerous internal bleeding.
  • Organ Damage: The liver, spleen, kidneys and lungs are particularly vulnerable to damage from blunt force trauma.
  • Concussions and Brain Injuries: A direct blow to the head or rapid movement within the skull can cause blunt force trauma to the head. This impact could lead to concussions or severe traumatic brain injuries.

Can Blunt Force Trauma Injuries Be Fatal?

Yes, blunt force trauma injuries can be fatal. Internal injuries that result in severe internal bleeding, organ damage or traumatic brain injuries are especially high-risk. Immediate medical attention is critical in such cases. Delayed treatment can lead to complications like shock, infection and multi-organ failure. These symptoms significantly increase the risk of a fatality.

What Symptoms May Indicate a Blunt Force Trauma Injury?

Symptoms of blunt force trauma can vary widely depending on the injury's location and severity. Common symptoms that accompany this type of injury may include:
  • Pain and tenderness at the site of impact
  • Swelling and bruising
  • Difficulty breathing or chest pain
  • Abdominal pain or distention
  • Dizziness, confusion or loss of consciousness (indicating possible brain injury)
  • Nausea and vomiting
  • Pale, clammy skin (a sign of internal bleeding or shock)

When Should I Seek Medical Attention After a Savannah Crash?

After a car crash in Savannah, it is crucial to seek medical attention immediately. This is true even if you do not see any injuries and even if you think you feel fine. Some injuries, especially internal ones, may not present symptoms right away. However, internal injuries, like those caused by blunt force trauma, can rapidly worsen without treatment.

Immediate Medical Attention Could Be Life-Saving

A thorough medical evaluation can identify internal bleeding, fractures, organ damage and other injuries. Taking this step right away can help to ensure you get care sooner than later. With severe injuries from blunt force trauma, this can make all the difference in your ability to recover.

Your Medical Records Help Link Your Injuries to the Crash

Since your injuries and hospital visit will be documented in your medical records, this step is also essential for any potential personal injury claim. This documentation is considered strong evidence, and it can help to link your injuries to the crash that caused them. It also provides evidence of the extent and severity of those injuries.
