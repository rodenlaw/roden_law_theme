## Usage notes

- Mix headlines across angles within one RSA; pin the brand/phone headline sparingly —
  over-pinning tanks Ad Strength.
- Angle 4 headlines pair best with road/neighborhood keyword ad groups if you split them.
- Descriptions swap freely within an angle.
- Phone: (843) 790-8999 · Office: 127 King Street · LP: /car-accident-lawyers/charleston-sc/

## Compliance notes (SC Rules of Professional Conduct 7.1–7.2)

- No outcome guarantees — "no fee unless we win" describes the fee, not the result.
- Spice-check before launch: **#20 "Adjusters Hate This Law Firm"** is meme-format puffery;
  most bar counsel treat obvious hyperbole as permissible, but it's the one to cut if the
  client's compliance reviewer is conservative. **#19 "300 Million Reasons"** is wordplay on
  a true aggregate figure — keep the "prior results do not guarantee a similar outcome"
  disclaimer on the landing page.
- Avoided "best"/comparative superlatives (unverifiable under Rule 7.1).
- Statute citations (§ 15-3-530, 25/50/25, Tort Claims Act notice) match the reviewed
  content record — don't alter in copy without updating the source.
